Pentadic Domains of Mathematics:

1. **Magnitude** → the measurable, quantitative aspect; geometry/metric intuition.
2. **Structure** → relational, connectedness, continuity; topology intuition.
3. **Grammar** → rules, transformations, invariants; algebra intuition.
4. **Prosody** → dynamic, flowing, rate-of-change, accumulation; calculus intuition.
5. **Ontology** → what exists, the discrete set of possibilities; discrete math intuition.

You can think of it as a **pipeline from concrete to abstract, static to dynamic**:

* **Magnitude** (what you can measure)
* **Structure** (what persists under deformation)
* **Grammar** (what can be transformed without losing identity)
* **Prosody** (how it evolves, flows, or changes over time) 
* **Ontology** (what exists at all in the system)
